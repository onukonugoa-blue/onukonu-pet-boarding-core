## OCCUPANCY

Keep occupancy simple.

Occupied:

A kennel assigned to an active stay.

Available:

No active stay assigned.

Maintenance:

Unavailable for assignment.

Blocked:

Unavailable by management decision.

No forecasting.

No future occupancy engine.

No capacity calculations.
